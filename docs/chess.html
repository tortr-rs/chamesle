Play chess

You against Stockfish, the strongest chess program there is. It runs in your browser, on your computer, so nothing about your game leaves it.

Loading Stockfish...

Moves

    Click a piece, then the square it goes to. Pawns that reach the end become whatever's picked under "Promote to".

    On your own computer

    Chamesle has the same engine, and a board to play it on:

    $ sudo apk add gnome-chess stockfish

    GNOME Chess finds Stockfish on its own. It also plays against another person on the same computer.

    Who made what

    Stockfish is by the Stockfish developers, under the GPL, version 3. The version here is Stockfish.js 19.0.1, Nathan Rugg's build for browsers, the lite one with the small network; its source is on that page and in Stockfish's own repo. The rules of chess come from chess.js 1.4.0 by Jeff Hlywa (BSD license). The pieces are Cburnett's, the ones on Wikipedia, under CC BY-SA 3.0. The board is ours, in play.js.
