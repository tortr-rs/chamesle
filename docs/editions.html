The old desktops

Chamesle has no desktops now.

The old Chamesle shipped Xfce, KDE Plasma, Cinnamon, MATE, GNOME, an astronomy flavor, a console one and a server one. Those were the old system's packages and they went with it. The new Chamesle is text mode only.

A desktop may come later. Nothing is planned. For news, see the news page.

Something wrong on this page? Tell us on the board.
Chamesle is installed by hand, from the handbook.
