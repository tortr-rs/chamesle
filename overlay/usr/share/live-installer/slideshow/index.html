Welcome to Chamesle

Thanks for choosing Chamesle. This will take a few minutes, here's a quick tour while you wait.

The start menu is bottom left, or press the Windows key. The taskbar and the clock are where you'd expect.

Apps

Flathub is already set up. Spotify, Discord, Steam, OBS, GIMP and thousands more are one command or one click away, and they update on their own.

flatpak install flathub com.spotify.Client

.deb and .rpm? Just double-click

Found an app that only comes for Ubuntu or Fedora? Download the .deb or .rpm and double-click it. pkgimport installs it with everything it needs.

In a terminal: sudo pkgimport install app.deb

Keeping up to date

Almost 15,000 packages come from the Chamesle repo, all signed. Update the whole system with:

sudo apk upgrade

and your Flathub apps with flatpak update.

Your computer, not ours

No telemetry, no ads, no account to sign into.

Drivers and firmware for most Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and graphics cards are already there. Printers are in Printers in the menu.
